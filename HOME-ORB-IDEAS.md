# Home-page ideas — orb + mystery, one at a time

Direction from the Jul 2026 home-page design panel. The through-line: the home should *reward curiosity* rather than explain itself — keep the orb and the withheld mood, but make a first-time visitor lean in. Do these focused and one at a time.

**Done: #1 — Reveal-on-approach + in-orb LA 2028 / OLYMPICS / countdown (simplified + retuned Jul 8 2026).** The orb reacts to the cursor from anywhere on the page and brightens/clarifies as it grows. **Size + growth (owner-tuned): `restScale` 0.875 (smallest, cursor in a far corner / off-page) → `peakScale` 1.33 (largest, cursor on the orb).** Growth is `restScale + range·t^proxExp` where `t`=0 at the farthest corner (so `reach` = ½ the diagonal for the centered orb) and `proxExp`=1.3 — i.e. ~10% closer from a corner ≈ 5% of the growth, and the rate keeps accelerating toward the orb (slow far, faster near). **The boat stays CENTERED and grows 1:1 with the orb (`boatGrow:1`, no rise).** The in-orb HUD is **three centred lines — `LA 2028` / `OLYMPICS` / countdown** — a coverage mask in the SHADER driven to the MOST-CONTRASTING monochrome per pixel (white over dark / black over light). **All three fade in TOGETHER on one curve tied to the orb's SIZE: begins at `hudRevealStart` (1.0×) and reaches full clarity at `hudRevealFull`×`peakScale` (0.95×) — full size, no grow-in.** Knobs in `glassOrbScene.js` TUNE: `restScale`, `peakScale`, `reach`, `proxExp`, `boatGrow`(1), `boatMaxScale`, `hudRevealStart`, `hudRevealFull`, `hudSize`, `hudTimerRatio`, `hudTitleGap`, `hudLineGap`, `hudTrack`, `hudWarp`, `hudAlpha`, `nearStrength`, `revealRest`, `revealPeak` (the old `boatRise`/`boatRiseAt`/`hudDrop`/`hudDropStart`/`hudLabelFloor`/`hudGrowEnd`/`hudStartScale`/`timerFloor` knobs were removed; morph-handoff damp is now ≈1). **Mobile keeps the LA 2028 HUD ABOVE the baked orb** (`MobileOrbHud`, `MainView.jsx`) — only the desktop live orb centers it over the boat, and the mobile orb size/reveal is baked (desktop-only tuning). Corner "LA 2028" + clock removed. See the branch `feat/orb-reveal-on-approach`.

**Also done Jul 8 2026 — Home reduced to one CTA.** The old small-grey bottom-left nav cluster (Bio · Team · Contact · Support · blurb) was replaced (single source of truth: `HOME_NAV` + `SupportArrow` in `MainView.jsx`) by a single, large, left-aligned **`Support` call-to-action** — the word plus a long arrow whose head lands at **85% of the viewport width** (padded from the left) — with the **blurb beneath it, brought out to `0.72` white** for legibility. It's **identical on mobile and every desktop width** and scales with the window (clamp). The four page links (Biography / The Team / The Road / Contact) were **removed from the home** — pages are reached via the **top hover-nav** (revealed on mouse-to-top; wide *and* narrow desktop, since the 6-item nav fits ≥700px so it never collapses) and, whenever the nav overflows (mobile, or any home where it would), the **hamburger** (App.jsx gate changed from `(!isHomeRoute || isMobile) && navOverflowing` to just `navOverflowing`). The orb still owns the cinematic route to The Road. This **supersedes** #2 (the blurb is now the prominent CTA sub-line, not a mumbled corner) and reverses #5 (the CTA is deliberately loud white). Earlier same-day iterations (a 4-link large-white bottom bar + narrow-reflow bands, then a mobile-only cinematic Support) are superseded by this.

## Deferred — for future focused passes

### #2 — Un-mumble the corner blurb
The bottom-left line already names US Sailing Team · Harvard · 2028, but at 0.4 opacity as a skippable run-on. Keep it a whisper but set it to be read once: nudge to ~0.55, break it on the same mid-dot the nav row uses (`… · … · …`), and give the lead clause with his name slightly more weight. It should state *less*, not more — an evocative lead plus fine-print credentials, no stats. Highest-leverage credibility move for near-zero cost. Guardrail: hold the lead to caption register, not a marketing tagline.

### #3 — The intro is the résumé
Re-curate the fast→slow flash montage so its frames read pre-attentively as world-level — a packed international start line, US Sailing Team kit, national colors — and make the already-held final latched frame (~2600ms, `HomeIntro`) the strongest of them. The montage is the one loud moment the design already sanctions, and it fires on every hard refresh; prime the stakes there and the withholding rest state reads as confidence, not emptiness. Guardrail: a start line or team kit, **not** a podium/trophy — the instant it's a glory-brag it tips into bragging. Pure curation, no engineering.

### #4 — Give the marble a pulse — **DONE (Jul 2026)**
Ambient life so the orb is visibly the one living thing in the dead space. Original idea was a rotation Lissajous "swim" + rim-glint crawl, but that can't be mirrored on the phone's flat baked-video orb without a re-bake — so (owner-directed) it shipped as a **breathing pulse** that is *identical* on desktop and mobile: a near-subliminal slow swell + interior glow (~6s cycle, scale +1.5%, brightness +6%), plus one deeper one-shot **"inhale"** the instant the page reaches `rest` (scale +3%, glow +10%, ~2.2s envelope), so a first-timer's eye lands on the orb as it wakes.
- **Desktop** (`glassOrbScene.js`): folded into the render loop — `pulse*`/`inhale*` TUNE knobs; breath multiplies `orb.scale` and adds to `uReveal` on TOP of the hover growth, **gated by `(1 − prox) × fadeIn`** so it bows out entirely as the cursor engages (at full hover the geometry is exactly as before — click zone untouched). One-shot `wake()` exposed on the scene → `orbOverlay.wake()` → cued from `MainView` when the orb goes visible at `rest`, module-gated (`orbWakePlayed`) so SPA re-entry never replays it.
- **Mobile** (`BakedOrb.jsx`): a single rAF writes `transform: scale()` + `filter: brightness()` to the rest `<video>` on the same rhythm (constants kept in sync with the TUNE block); the inhale fires on the first `playing` event; frozen during the morph, skipped for reduced motion. No re-bake needed — the tap hotspot is a separate circle, so it's unmoved.
- Verified (puppeteer): mobile video breath measured at scale 1.000→1.015 / brightness 1.000→1.060, inhale peak 1.034 / 1.116 ~0.8s after load; desktop reaches `rest` error-free, the hover reveal still blooms, and the orb click still navigates to `/the-road` (click zone intact).
- Guardrails kept: amplitude near-subliminal; **never** touches `orbFrac`/position; the one-shot wake is cued from MainView at the rest transition and gated against replay.

### #5 — Quiet the chrome into one system
Collapse the ~5 mismatched greys (nav 0.75, blurb 0.4, dots 0.35, etc.) onto a 3-step tonal ramp stepped down from the shader's own `FRESNEL_COLOR` (≈rgb 158,184,219), so text and glass share one cast; give any remaining figures `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. Cohesion reads as intention — the difference between half-finished UI and a composed page. Reveals nothing new; just harmonizes. (Note: #1 removed the corner countdown, so re-scope this to whatever chrome remains.)
