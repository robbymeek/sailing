# Chart data licences

The files in this folder are generated by `scripts/charts/bake.mjs`.

## Venue charts (`<place>.json`)

Coastline, water and place-name data © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors, available under the [Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

Read through the [OpenMapTiles](https://openmaptiles.org/) vector tile schema, served by [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/). The simplified geometry in these files is a derivative of the OpenStreetMap database and is itself made available under the ODbL 1.0.

## World outline (`_world.json`)

Land outline from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) 1:110m (public domain), via the [world-atlas](https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas) package.
